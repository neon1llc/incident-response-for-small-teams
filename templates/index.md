---
title: Templates
description: "Fill-in forms for the plan, the contact sheet, the incident log, notification decisions, after-action reports, and tabletop records."
---

Copy these into your own documents and fill the blanks. An empty field is an open question to settle before you need the answer. Print the plan and the contact sheet, since the computers that hold them may be the ones that are down.

| Template | Use it | Filled in by |
|---|---|---|
| [Response plan](response-plan.md) | Before anything happens. Review yearly | The owner |
| [Contact sheet](contact-sheet.md) | Before anything happens. Review twice a year | The incident lead |
| [Incident log](incident-log.md) | From the first report until the incident closes | The scribe |
| [Notification worksheet](notification-worksheet.md) | Once personal information may be involved | The incident lead, with the lawyer |
| [After-action report](after-action-report.md) | Within two weeks of closing | The incident lead |
| [Tabletop record](tabletop-record.md) | At the end of each exercise | The facilitator |

Kept together, these are the records insurers, auditors, and regulators ask for: a written plan, proof it's practiced, and a record of each incident from report to lessons learned. SOC 2 Readiness lists what a SOC 2 auditor asks for in its [incident evidence checklist](https://github.com/neon1llc/soc-2-readiness/blob/main/evidence/incidents.md).
