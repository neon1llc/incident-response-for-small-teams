---
title: Templates
description: "Fill-in forms for the plan, contacts, incident log, notification decisions, after-action reports, and tabletop records."
---

Copy these into your own documents and fill the blanks. An empty field is an open question to settle before you need the answer. Print the plan and the contact sheet, since the computers holding them may be down.

| Template | Use it | Filled in by |
|---|---|---|
| [Response plan](response-plan.md) | Before anything happens. Review yearly | The owner |
| [Contact sheet](contact-sheet.md) | Before anything happens. Review twice a year | The incident lead |
| [Incident log](incident-log.md) | From the first report to closing | The scribe |
| [Notification worksheet](notification-worksheet.md) | Once personal information may be involved | The lead, with the lawyer |
| [After-action report](after-action-report.md) | Within two weeks of closing | The incident lead |
| [Tabletop record](tabletop-record.md) | After each exercise | The facilitator |

Insurers, auditors, and regulators ask for these records: a written plan, evidence it's practiced, and a record of each incident. SOC 2 Readiness lists what an auditor asks for in its [incident evidence checklist](https://github.com/neon1llc/soc-2-readiness/blob/main/evidence/incidents.md).
