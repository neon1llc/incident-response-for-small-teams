---
title: Glossary
description: "Plain definitions of the incident response terms used in this kit."
---

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **After-action review** | A blameless meeting after an incident or exercise on what worked, what didn't, and what will change. Right after an exercise it's called a hotwash. See [learn](../runbook/learn.md) |
| **Breach** | An incident in which personal information was, or may have been, seen or taken by someone unauthorized. Each law defines it differently. See [is this an incident?](../runbook/is-this-an-incident.md) |
| **Breach coach** | A lawyer, usually assigned by a cyber insurer, who coordinates the response and decides on notification |
| **Chain of custody** | A record of who collected each piece of evidence, when, and where it's been kept since |
| **Discovery** | The moment the business knew, or should have known, about an incident. Most notification deadlines count from here |
| **Forensics** | Investigation of devices, accounts, and logs to establish what happened, usually by a specialist firm |
| **Inject** | A new piece of information a facilitator adds during a tabletop exercise |
