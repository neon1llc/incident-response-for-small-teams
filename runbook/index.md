## The roles

Every incident has these four jobs. One person may hold two, but the lead and the scribe should differ.

| Role | Does |
|---|---|
| **Incident lead** (usually the owner) | Declares the incident, sets priorities, decides: disconnect, notify, spend |
| **Tech lead** (the IT person or managed service provider, MSP) | Contains, investigates, and restores |
| **Scribe** (anyone calm and careful) | Keeps the [incident log](../templates/incident-log.md) with times |
| **Communications** | Talks to staff, clients, and outsiders with wording the lead and lawyer approved |

Outside help (the insurer's breach coach, a lawyer, a forensics firm) advises. The incident lead decides.
