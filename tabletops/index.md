---
title: Tabletop exercises
description: "Six tabletop exercises of 60 to 90 minutes, built around the incidents small businesses see most."
---

A tabletop exercise is a "what would we do if...?" conversation. Nobody touches a real system. The facilitator reveals a scenario in stages, and the team talks through what they'd know, do, and decide. It's the cheapest way to learn that the insurer's number lives only in the mailbox you'd lose first.

Start with the [facilitator guide](facilitator-guide.md). Each exercise matches a [playbook](../playbooks/index.md), so the team can check its answers afterward. Each has three or four injects with questions, what good looks like, and a debrief that ends in the [tabletop record](../templates/tabletop-record.md).

| # | Exercise | Scenario |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [The wire that wasn't](01-the-wire-that-wasnt.md) | A supplier's "new bank details" email, and a payment that never arrives |
| 2 | [Monday morning ransom](02-monday-morning-ransom.md) | Files locked across the office, and a ransom note |
| 3 | [Left in the cab](03-left-in-the-cab.md) | A laptop with client files disappears |
| 4 | [The forwarding rule](04-the-forwarding-rule.md) | A staff mailbox has quietly forwarded mail for three weeks |
| 5 | [Our vendor got hacked](05-our-vendor-got-hacked.md) | A tax, practice management, or IT provider announces a breach |
| 6 | [Reply all](06-reply-all.md) | A spreadsheet of client Social Security numbers goes to the wrong list |

Run one at least yearly, and after a big change to staff, systems, or vendors. Rotate scenarios and who plays incident lead. CISA's Tabletop Exercise Packages and the UK NCSC's Exercise in a Box have more scenarios.

## Sources

- [CISA Tabletop Exercise Packages](https://www.cisa.gov/resources-tools/services/cisa-tabletop-exercise-packages), from CISA: free scenario packages and the exercise format these follow.
- [Exercise in a Box](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/section/exercise-in-a-box/overview), from the UK National Cyber Security Centre: free exercises for small organizations.
- [Guide to Test, Training, and Exercise Programs](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/84/final), NIST SP 800-84: designing, running, and evaluating tabletop exercises.
- [Practice a bad day with a tabletop exercise](https://github.com/neon1llc/ready-secure-go/blob/main/content/practices/respond-and-recover/resp-tabletop-exercise.md), in Ready, Secure, Go: the short version for any group.
