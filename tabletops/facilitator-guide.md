title: Facilitator guide
description: "How to prepare, run, and close a tabletop exercise for a small team: roles, ground rules, a 90-minute run sheet, and the hotwash."
---

**Length:** 60 to 90 minutes. **Group:** 3 to 12 people. **You need:** a room or video call, a printed copy of the exercise for yourself, printed [response plan](../templates/response-plan.md) and [contact sheet](../templates/contact-sheet.md) for the table, and a note-taker.

## Before the day

1. **Pick the exercise** that matches a real risk for this business. If unsure, start with [the wire that wasn't](01-the-wire-that-wasnt.md).
2. **Agree on two or three objectives** with the owner, such as "find out whether we can reach the insurer without email" or "decide who approves disconnecting the office."
3. **Make it local.** Swap in the business's real systems, vendors, and job titles, such as "QuickBooks" or "Drake" for "the accounting system."
4. **Invite the people who'd be involved in a real incident:** the owner, the person who handles money, whoever runs IT (or the MSP), and whoever talks to clients. Small teams can include everyone.
5. **Don't share the scenario in advance.** Share the date, the length, and that it's a discussion, not a test.

## Roles in the room

| Role | Does |
|---|---|
| **Facilitator** | Reads the injects, asks the questions, keeps time, and keeps it moving. Doesn't give answers during play |
| **Note-taker** | Records decisions, gaps, and questions nobody could answer, on the [tabletop record](../templates/tabletop-record.md) |
| **Players** | Answer as they would today, with what they have today |
| **Observer** (optional) | Watches one thing, such as communication, and reports at the hotwash |

## Ground rules to read out

- This is practice. Nobody is being graded, and "I don't know" is a useful answer.
- Answer with what we have now, not what we wish we had.
- Assume the scenario is real, even if it seems unlikely.
- Decisions made today aren't binding. Gaps found today are the point.

## Run sheet (90 minutes)

| Time | Segment |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Welcome, objectives, ground rules |
| 0:10 | Inject 1, and discussion |
| 0:25 | Inject 2, and discussion |
| 0:45 | Inject 3, and discussion |
| 1:05 | Inject 4, if time allows |
| 1:15 | Hotwash |
| 1:30 | Close: the three fixes, their owners, and dates |

For 60 minutes, use three injects.

## Facilitating well

- **Ask, don't tell.** "Who would do that?" "How would you reach them?" "Where's that number written down?"
- **Push for specifics.** "We'd call IT" becomes "Who calls, on what number, and what if it's 7pm Friday?"
- **Let silence work.** The gap is often the finding.
- **Note gaps without solving them.** Write them down and move on.
- **Keep one voice from dominating.** Ask quieter people directly, especially whoever would notice first.

## The hotwash

Spend the last 15 minutes on these questions, then go around the table so everyone speaks:

1. What surprised you?
2. What would have gone wrong first if this were real?
3. What do we need that we don't have?
4. What are the three fixes we'll make, who owns each, and by when?

Within a week, send the completed [tabletop record](../templates/tabletop-record.md) to everyone and put the fixes on the next staff meeting agenda. Keep the record, because an insurer or auditor may ask for it.
