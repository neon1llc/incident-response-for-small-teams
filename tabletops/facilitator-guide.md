title: Facilitator guide
description: "How to prepare, run, and close a tabletop exercise: roles, ground rules, a run sheet, and the hotwash."
---

**Length:** 60 to 90 minutes. **Group:** 3 to 12 people. **You need:** a room or video call, a printed copy of the exercise for yourself, printed [response plan](../templates/response-plan.md) and [contact sheet](../templates/contact-sheet.md) for the table, and a note-taker.

## Before the day

1. **Pick the exercise** that matches a real risk for this business. If unsure, start with [the wire that wasn't](01-the-wire-that-wasnt.md).
2. **Agree on two or three objectives** with the owner, such as "find out whether we can reach the insurer without email."
3. **Make it local.** Swap in the business's real systems, vendors, and job titles.
4. **Invite the people who'd be involved in a real incident:** the owner, whoever handles money, whoever runs IT (or the MSP), and whoever talks to clients.
5. **Don't share the scenario in advance.**

## Roles

| Role | Does |
|---|---|
| **Facilitator** | Reads the injects, asks the questions, keeps time. Doesn't give answers |
| **Note-taker** | Records decisions, gaps, and unanswered questions on the [tabletop record](../templates/tabletop-record.md) |
| **Players** | Answer as they would today |


## Ground rules to read out

This is practice, and "I don't know" is a useful answer. Assume the scenario is real, and answer with what we have now. Decisions made today aren't binding. Gaps found today are the point.

## Run sheet (90 minutes)

| Time | Segment |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Welcome, objectives, ground rules |
| 0:10 | Inject 1, and discussion |
| 0:25 | Inject 2, and discussion |
| 0:45 | Inject 3, and discussion |
| 1:05 | Inject 4, if there is one |
| 1:15 | Hotwash |
| 1:30 | Close |

For 60 minutes, use three injects.

## Facilitating well

- **Ask, don't tell.** "Who would do that? Where's that number written down?"
- **Push for specifics.** "We'd call IT" becomes "Who calls, on what number, and what if it's 7pm Friday?"
- **Write gaps down and move on.** The gap is often the finding.
- **Ask quieter people directly,** especially whoever would notice first.

## The hotwash

Spend the last 15 minutes on these questions, going around the table so everyone speaks:

1. What surprised you?
2. What would have gone wrong first if this were real?
3. What do we need that we don't have?
4. What are the three fixes we'll make, who owns each, and by when?

Within a week, send the completed [tabletop record](../templates/tabletop-record.md) to everyone and put the fixes on the next staff meeting agenda. Keep the record, because an insurer or auditor may ask for it.
